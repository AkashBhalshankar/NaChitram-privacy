# ScioFlow Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 22, 2026**

ScioFlow is a Chrome extension that records your screen, webcam and microphone. This policy explains what happens to that information.

**Short version: ScioFlow does not collect, sell or receive your data. Recordings and project data stay on your computer. If you enable optional Live Captions, Chrome's speech-recognition service may process microphone audio under Google's terms; ScioFlow never receives that audio or transcript.**

## What ScioFlow processes

| Information | Why | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screen, window or tab video and audio you choose to record | To make your recording | Processed in your browser. Saved on your computer. |
| Camera and microphone | To show your camera bubble and record your voice | Processed in your browser. Never transmitted. |
| Whiteboard drawings, typed text and sticky notes | To annotate the screen, recover an interrupted canvas and export PNG/JSON/notes files | Kept in your browser; recovery data is cleared after a normal recording stop. |
| Teleprompter script and settings (selected devices, ink colour, speed) | To remember your preferences | Stored in the browser's local extension storage on your device. |
| The last three recordings | So you can preview and download after you stop | Stored in the browser's local database (IndexedDB) on your device until you delete them or newer ones replace them. |
| Click labels and timestamps recorded during an active session | To create local chapters and a step-by-step guide | Stored with that local recording and never sent to ScioFlow. |
| Temporary screenshots, privacy shields, cursor effects and virtual-background masks | To render the presenter tools you select | Processed locally in the browser and not uploaded by ScioFlow. |

## What ScioFlow does not do

- It does not upload recordings, screenshots, drawings, scripts or project files to a ScioFlow server.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, cookies or fingerprinting.
- It does not create accounts or ask for your name or email.
- It does not load executable code or AI models from the internet; MediaPipe and its segmentation model are bundled with the extension and run on the device.
- It does not read or collect the content of websites you visit. It only draws on the tab you choose to record, while you record it.

## Permissions

ScioFlow asks Chrome for these permissions and uses them only for the features described: `activeTab`, `scripting`, `storage`, `unlimitedStorage`, `downloads`, `tabCapture`, `offscreen`, and `windows`. Your screen, camera and microphone are only used after you grant access in Chrome's own prompts.

## Optional Live Captions

Live Captions is off by default. When you turn it on, ScioFlow requests transcription through Chrome's built-in Web Speech recognition interface. Depending on Chrome, the operating system and language settings, microphone audio may be sent by Chrome to Google's speech-recognition service for processing. ScioFlow does not operate that service, does not receive the audio, and does not transmit the resulting caption text to a ScioFlow server. Do not enable Live Captions if you require fully offline speech processing.

## The optional support card

After you save a recording, ScioFlow may show a voluntary UPI support card. The QR code is generated entirely on your device from the configured public UPI ID. ScioFlow does not open a payment website and never sees or handles any payment.

## Your control

- Delete any recording from the summary page, or remove the extension to delete all of ScioFlow's stored data.
- You can revoke camera and microphone access any time at `chrome://settings/content`.
- Files you download are normal files on your computer; ScioFlow does not keep track of them.

## Children

ScioFlow is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

## Consent of other people

You are responsible for getting consent from anyone who appears or speaks in your recordings. ScioFlow shows a reminder the first time you open it.

## Your rights (India DPDP Act 2023 and similar laws)

ScioFlow does not collect or hold personal data on any server, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase remotely. All data is on your device and under your control. Use **Delete all my ScioFlow data** in the popup's settings to erase every recording, draft, template and setting at once.
